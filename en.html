The stars light up one by one

After each star lights up, a puzzle interrupts you. After the last round, click every star that lit up, in the order it appeared.

Keys: ← or F for True, → or J for False
━ Correct order┅ Your order

Pick a difficulty, then press Start.
How to play: each round one star lights up for about a second, then a true/false puzzle interrupts you (compare sums, number sequences, logic, shape rotation, symmetry; 12.5 to 16 seconds each). Then you return to the sky and the next star lights up. After the last round, click the stars in the order they lit. Your score is how many positions you got exactly right; your puzzle accuracy is shown too. Top right is your health bar: every wrong or timed-out puzzle summons a mocking clown and costs one block. When wrong answers reach 30% of the round count, the game ends.
